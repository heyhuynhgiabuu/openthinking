# ICP Pain Map Examples

## Example invocation

> Run `icp-pain-map` for a product that helps founders stop wasting time on weak SaaS ideas.
>
> Idea brief: an AI-guided system that pressures solo builders to narrow, price, and validate ideas before building.
> Market guess: indie hackers, solo founders, and small bootstrapped teams.
> Known user examples: people who repeatedly start projects, buy courses, and abandon products after 2-6 weeks.

## Example output

### Primary ICP
Solo builders who repeatedly start new SaaS ideas but struggle to choose one, narrow it, and commit long enough to test it properly.

### Urgent pain
They waste weeks or months building broad, weak, or badly positioned products because they do not have a disciplined way to reject bad ideas early.

### Current workaround
- asking friends or Twitter for opinions
- journaling and making vague pros/cons lists
- buying courses or templates
- building first and hoping clarity appears later
- bouncing between ideas whenever motivation drops

### Why now
AI lowers the cost of building, which increases the risk of building the wrong thing faster. Solo builders need judgment systems more now, not less.

### Rejected ICPs
- **VC-backed startups:** wrong fit; they have different decision dynamics and team structure.
- **Agencies:** may have adjacent problems, but not the same repeated founder decision loop.
- **All founders:** too broad and strategically weak.
- **Creators in general:** vague audience, weak pain specificity.

### Recommended next skill
`wedge-positioning`

## Why this is a good example

It chooses one ICP instead of hiding behind “all founders,” and it links the pain to a real repeated behavior pattern rather than abstract ambition.
