# GTM Channel Picker

## Purpose

Use this skill to choose one practical distribution channel for an early product.

## When to use

Use when the product wedge and likely ICP are clear enough to choose an acquisition motion.

## Required inputs

- ICP
- wedge
- product type
- founder strengths
- available assets

## Output contract

Return these sections:

1. `Primary channel`
2. `Why this channel fits`
3. `Why other common channels are weaker right now`
4. `30-day GTM plan`
5. `Metrics to track`

## Rules

- pick one primary channel
- match the channel to founder strengths and product shape
- avoid multi-channel sprawl

## Stop rules

Stop when there is one channel and one short execution plan.
