# Idea Scorecard Examples

## Example invocation

> Use `idea-scorecard` on this idea: a solo-builder tool that helps indie founders validate SaaS ideas before building.
>
> Builder strengths: product thinking, writing, frontend engineering.
> Time budget: 10 hours/week for 8 weeks.
> Target customer guess: solo builders and indie hackers.
> Business model guess: freemium or low-cost subscription.

## Example output

### Idea summary
A decision-support product for solo builders that helps them judge startup ideas before wasting months building weak products.

### Scorecard
- **Pain severity:** 7/10  
  Wasting months on the wrong project is a real pain for solo builders.
- **Urgency:** 5/10  
  Pain is real, but many builders procrastinate or tolerate it.
- **Adoption friction:** 4/10  
  Easy to try if the workflow is lightweight and artifact-driven.
- **Founder-fit:** 8/10  
  Strong fit if the builder is good at product thinking and sharp written judgment.
- **Distribution plausibility:** 7/10  
  Reasonable via content, GitHub, founder communities, and AI-skill distribution.
- **Monetization plausibility:** 5/10  
  Possible, but many builders expect advice to be free unless the output is operationally useful.
- **Defensibility:** 4/10  
  Weak if it becomes generic advice; stronger if it becomes a reusable skill system with saved artifacts and repeat usage.

### Top risks
1. The product becomes motivational content instead of a repeatable decision tool.
2. The target audience is too broad and cheap, which weakens monetization.
3. Users may use it once for curiosity and never come back.

### Assumptions to test
- Solo builders want structured decision artifacts, not just a chat response.
- At least some users will return weekly for project review, not just idea validation.
- Clear, blunt outputs are more valuable than encouraging brainstorming.

### Recommendation
**Narrow**

The idea is promising, but the audience and product framing are too broad. Do not launch it as a generic “founder copilot.” Narrow it to a brutal decision system for solo builders and optimize for repeatable artifacts.

### Recommended next skill
`icp-pain-map`

## Why this is a good example

It does not just say “good idea.” It makes a real judgment, identifies monetization and retention risk, and points clearly to the next narrowing step.
